Privacy Policy — Magna-Knight

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Magna-Knight is a single-player game. The current version does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data through the game.

Game progress, scores, settings, and unlocked items are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may delete this information. The game does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

The game can be played offline. If future versions add online services or data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before release.
